## Full Report Structure (for later reviews)

Preliminary Pages → Ch.1 Introduction → Ch.2 Literature/Patent Review → Ch.3 Methodology/System Design → Ch.4 Implementation & Results → Ch.5 Conclusion & Future Work → References → Appendices.

## General Guidelines Reminders

- Max 3 members per team; maintain evidence of guide meetings, feedback, and action taken.
- Every review comment + action taken must be recorded and shown at the next review.
- Individual contribution must be clearly demonstrable; each student submits an individual final report.
- No plagiarism/unauthorized AI content without acknowledgement — significant AI tool use must be disclosed.
- PPT must be guide-approved before the panel review (mark entry only opens after approval in the portal).
- Any deviation from attending a review must be informed to the HoD before the review start time.
